Don’t idolize authentic products! Even genuine items worth 20,000 yuan are handmade. Sometimes a single bag shows mismatched colors, yet it’s still authentic—you should accept that. Velvet fabric from the original factory often has color variations on each piece of leather. The original leather we receive has the same issue. We can only select the usable parts; unusable ones are discarded. It’s common to use one piece of leather for just one bag. Original leather carries high risk—sometimes you get a good batch, sometimes the entire batch must be scrapped after production.
