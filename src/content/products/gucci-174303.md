Currently, I found that there are five factories abroad making leather for Gucc*, with four of them doing OEM work. 1) Different factories will definitely produce leather with color variations (even batches from the same factory have color differences). The patterns and methods also vary slightly, even though they use the same templates, as different craftsmen add subtle differences. 2) Frequent buyers of authentic products notice that the internal codes often differ, indicating production from different factories. So, some minor details aren’t worth obsessing over. If you keep chasing changes, you’ll find that by the time you finish, the store’s current stock has already changed again!
