Burberry's new tote bag is crafted from Italian imported grained calfskin leather, featuring a cotton blend lining with the iconic Burberry check pattern in sand color. The exquisite design incorporates check-embossed closure and ring hardware, inspired by the waist belt design of the brand's classic Trench coat. It can be carried by hand or worn over the shoulder with the detachable strap, measuring 24.5×13×19.5cm. Model number 349170.
