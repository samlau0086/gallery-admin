The latest Balenciaga Crush bag debuts powerfully! First introduced in the Winter 22 collection, the Crush handbag features a unique signature curved shape with a smooth, rounded arch at the base, reinterpreting the classic through deconstruction. Its flexible design allows easy folding and unfolding. The arched base contrasts with sharp edges, complemented by a layered interior space. Made of polished calfskin with a soft lambskin lining, the bag’s texture and design details highlight its exceptional touch. Dimensions: 31.0 cm L x 19.8 cm H x 6.9 cm W.
