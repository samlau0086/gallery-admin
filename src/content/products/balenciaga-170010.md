Balenciaga New Horizontal Camera Motorcycle Bag 📷  
The ever-popular Balenciaga motorcycle bag is back with a new design!

Features adjustable woven shoulder strap, extra eyelets, an external leather card holder, and replaces the heart-shaped mirror with an AirPods holder—simply perfect!  
Unisex design.
