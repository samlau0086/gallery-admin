Original hardware and leather, easily pass customs and counters. Sweet or cool style~  
Goyard mini Saigon bag. Since our last launch of the Goyard mini tote, many have requested this Saigon bag. The wooden handle is uniquely attractive.  
This highly sought-after mini Saigon bag symbolizes love, elegance, and refinement. Low-key with perfect craftsmanship, it’s a charming vintage style that’s hard to resist.  
The wooden handle is a stylish highlight, adding romantic elegance. The shoulder strap is fully detachable, allowing it to be carried by the small wooden ring—super chic!  
Like the authentic, the wood grain on the ring is clear and smooth, treated with imported waterproof lacquer to prevent yellowing or moisture damage over time.  
The Saigon bag uses its printed logo sparingly, only under the flap. Leather covers the edges for durability and a refined finish.  
Size: 20*16*6 cm. Large capacity, versatile for all seasons.
