Limited edition collaboration design by BVLG** featuring the iconic print queen designer Mary Katrantzou's snake head bag 🐍 is simply stunning. Redefining the snake head bag with a new interpretation, it is crafted with soft matte crocodile embossed lambskin paired with light gold-plated brass accents. The light gold-plated brass reinterprets the classic SERPENTI snake head clasp, adorned with captivating crystal eyes, showcasing elements from BVLGARI's antique watches. The interior features an open pocket, inner pocket, and a metal plaque engraved with "MARY KATRANTZOU X BVLGARI" logo. It comes with a detachable snake-shaped handle and a removable chain shoulder strap, allowing it to be worn as a crossbody bag, versatile and stylish. Size: 19.2*15*6cm.
