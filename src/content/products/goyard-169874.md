This waterproof nylon with leather trim from the Le Pliage Cuir series features neat embroidery and stitching, a vintage-style crossbody messenger design, moderate size, suitable for all genders. Matches well with sweatshirts and trench coats. Dimensions: 36/27/14 cm.
