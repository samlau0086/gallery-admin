B brand's latest Strand stitching clutch/backpack is crafted from small cowhide leather with a unique process that gives it a soft and flexible textured surface. Adorned with a metal knight emblem, inspired by the archival pattern of the brand's bags from the 1980s. It can be carried by hand or worn over the shoulder with a lightweight chain strap to create a crossbody style. The chain shoulder strap can be worn in multiple ways - top flap with magnetic closure, 4 card slots, front engraved with equestrian knight emblem (EKD). Dimensions: 19 x 5.5 x 13cm. Model: 641165.
