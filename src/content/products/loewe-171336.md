New version of Puzzle Edge handbag by Loewe features soft grained calfskin leather with a unique geometric design created by precise cutting techniques. This mini version is made of soft grained calfskin leather with overlapping edges. It can fit a small vertical wallet, most smartphones (up to 6.1 inches), sunglasses, and can be worn over the shoulder, crossbody, handheld, or with the top handle. The shoulder strap is adjustable and detachable. It has an interior slip pocket, an exterior back zip pocket, a leather zip pull, and a cotton canvas lining with Anagram embossing. Size: 18*12.5*8 cm. Model: 06231272.
