Burberry's new tote bag is crafted from Italian imported grained calfskin leather, featuring a cotton blend lining with the iconic sand-colored Burberry check pattern. The exquisite design incorporates check-embossed closure and ring hardware inspired by the brand's classic Trench coat belt design. It can be carried by hand or worn over the shoulder with the detachable shoulder strap, measuring 24.5×13×19.5cm. Model number: 343177.
