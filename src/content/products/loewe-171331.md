Introducing the new version of Puzzle Edge handbag by Loewe, featuring soft grain leather and a unique geometric design created by precise cutting techniques. This mini version is crafted from soft grain leather with overlapping edges, capable of holding a small vertical wallet, most smartphones (up to 6.1 inches), sunglasses, and can be worn over the shoulder, crossbody, handheld, or with the top handle. It comes with an adjustable and detachable shoulder strap, an interior slip pocket, an exterior zip pocket at the back, a leather zipper pull, and a cotton canvas lining with Anagram embossing. Size: 18*12.5*8 cm. Model: 06231272.
