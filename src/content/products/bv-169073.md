🟡🟡🟡Bottega Veneta's latest Ciao Ciao bag features smooth calf leather with a dual-flap design, adorned with the iconic twisted knot detail. It has a hidden hook closure that can secure the front flap or connect to the INTRECCIATO woven inner pocket. The bag comes with an adjustable and detachable crossbody strap, internal compartments, and a zip pocket. The surprise flap design offers versatility: Style 1 - covering the bag face, revealing the knot decoration; Style 2 - placing the flap inside the bag with a loop; Style 3 - holding the top handle in a U-shape position inside the bag. Style number: 835835. Dimensions: 26x19x10.5cm.
