Introducing the new version of Puzzle Edge handbag by Loewe, featuring soft grainy calfskin leather and a unique geometric design created by precise cutting techniques. This small size version is adorned with overlapping edges and can fit a large wallet, all sizes of phones, sunglasses, and can be worn over the shoulder, crossbody, handheld, or with the top handle. It comes with an adjustable and detachable shoulder strap, an interior slip pocket, an exterior zip pocket at the back, and a leather zipper pull with the brand's Anagram embossed on it. Lined with herringbone cotton canvas. Dimensions: 24*10.5*16CM. Model: 06232485.
