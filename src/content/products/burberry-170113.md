B brand's latest soft and structured tote bag is crafted from grained calfskin leather. Made with precision in an Italian workshop, it features a suede lining. The bag incorporates the brand's iconic b-shaped closure, with smooth calfskin leather panels adorned with an embossed equestrian knight emblem (EKD). It comes with a detachable shoulder strap for versatile styling as a handbag or shoulder bag. The back is embellished with an embossed equestrian knight emblem (EKD). Dimensions: 35.5 × 18 × 28cm. Model number: 575190.
