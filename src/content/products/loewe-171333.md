Introducing the new version of the Puzzle Edge handbag from the brand Black Lychee Pattern. This mini version is crafted from soft grainy cowhide leather with overlapping edges, creating unique geometric lines. It can fit items such as a small vertical wallet, most smartphones (up to 6.1 inches), sunglasses, and can be carried on the shoulder, crossbody, or by hand. Features an adjustable and detachable shoulder strap, an internal slip pocket, an external back zip pocket, and a leather zipper pull with the brand's Anagram embossed. Lined with herringbone cotton canvas. Size: 18*12.5*8 cm. Model: 062312 F. Price: 580 (comes with full packaging).
