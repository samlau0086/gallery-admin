Top-tier shopping bag series with high-definition copper badge. The reversible design of the top-grade original MCM allows for the use of either the checkered pattern or floral print lining. The adjustable drawstring on the side can modify the silhouette, and it comes with a detachable inner bag. Features top leather double handles, side drawstrings to tighten the opening, a detachable zippered storage pouch inside, and a shoulder strap. The latest large-size double-sided shopping bag, Visetos Liz, with a mother-and-child single-shoulder bag. Dimensions: Large shopping bag 35*29*16, small mother single-shoulder bag 29*21.
