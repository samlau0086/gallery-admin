Loewe's new version of the Puzzle Edge handbag features a soft grained calfskin leather in a cube shape with precise cutting techniques, creating unique geometric lines. This mini version is made of soft grained calfskin leather with overlapping edges. It can fit a small vertical wallet, most smartphones (up to 6.1 inches), sunglasses, and can be carried on the shoulder, crossbody, handheld, or with the top handle. The shoulder strap is adjustable and detachable. It has an interior slip pocket, an exterior back zip pocket, a leather zip pull, and a cotton canvas lining with Anagram embossing. Size: 18*12.5*8 cm. Model: 06231272.
