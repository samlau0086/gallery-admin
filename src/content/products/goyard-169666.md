Longchamp Replay 3.0 Shoulder & Tote Bag

The dopamine-inspired color-blocked small crossbody tote is stunning and effortlessly photogenic! The Longchamp Replay 3.0 shoulder bag comes in six colorways, each combining nylon canvas with a contrasting lambskin, accented by lambskin piping and black Russian cowhide handles. Like a spilled palette, it creatively showcases French aesthetic with bright colors and unique styles.

Featuring vibrant tones, sharp cuts, and a classic shape, it exudes sporty optimism. Eye-catching and youthful, it’s a must-have for dopamine girls’ wardrobes, perfect for adding lively color to summer outfits. Pair it with a small charm for an energetic, cute vibe that’s natural and vibrant.

The bag is spacious enough for daily essentials like phone, umbrella, wallet, keys, and lipstick. A lambskin patch pocket on the body holds small items securely.

Colors: Pink, Beige, Magenta, Army Green, Navy Blue, Sky Blue  
Size: 17 x 7.5 x 23.5 cm
