Regarding the authentic mandarin duck color, I think it’s quite normal. One piece of nubuck leather can yield over three colors. I can’t be as free as the original to cut anywhere. We have to carefully select areas with matching colors. Edges, corners, and parts with different colors or poor leather quality are usually unusable. In the end, one hide can barely make three bags.
