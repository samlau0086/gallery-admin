Loewe's new Puzzle Fold mini grain leather handbag draws inspiration from the geometric lines of the brand's classic handbag series. This mini version is made of grain leather, with options for shoulder, crossbody, or hand carry. It comes with a detachable and adjustable shoulder strap, suede lining, and Loewe embossed logo. Size: 16.5*9.5*20CM. Model: 05232270.
