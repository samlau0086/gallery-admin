Goyard mini Saigon bag. Since our last launch of the Goyard mini tote, many have requested this Saigon bag. The wooden handle is uniquely attractive.  
This highly sought-after mini Saigon bag symbolizes love, elegance, and refinement. Low-key with perfect craftsmanship. Elegant, vintage, and charming—hard to resist.  
The wooden handle is a stylish highlight, adding romance and elegance. The shoulder strap is fully detachable; carry by the small wooden ring for an ultra-chic look.  
Like the authentic, the wood grain on the ring is clear and smooth, treated with imported waterproof lacquer to prevent yellowing or moisture damage over time.  
Saigon uses its signature print sparingly, only under the flap. Leather covers the edges for durability and refined workmanship.  
Size: 20*16*6cm. Large capacity, versatile for all seasons.
