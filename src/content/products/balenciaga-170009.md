Balenciaga New Motorcycle Bag 🏍 The ever-popular Balenciaga motorcycle bag is back with a new design! Improved from the classic model, it retains iconic motorcycle elements with a bold, rock-inspired edge. Spacious enough for daily essentials, it even fits an iPhone Max. Features adjustable woven shoulder strap with extra eyelets, an external leather card holder, and the heart-shaped mirror replaced by an AirPods holder—simply perfect! Suitable for both men and women. Size: 21cm H x 17cm W x 4.5cm D.
